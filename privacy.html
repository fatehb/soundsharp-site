Sound Sharp Support Privacy Terms

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Sound Sharp collects the following information when you use our app:

  • Account information (email address, display name)
  • Audio recordings you submit for analysis
  • Speech analysis results (transcripts, scores, feedback)
  • Usage data (session frequency, streak data)
  • Device information (for push notifications)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide speech analysis and coaching feedback
  • Track your progress and maintain streaks
  • Generate personalized vocabulary suggestions
  • Send practice reminder notifications (if enabled)
  • Improve our AI coaching models

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to process your data:

  • Firebase — authentication, data storage, cloud functions
  • OpenAI Whisper — speech-to-text transcription
  • AssemblyAI — speech analysis and filler word detection
  • Anthropic Claude — AI coaching feedback generation

Audio recordings are processed in real-time and are not retained by third-party AI services beyond the processing period.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely in Firebase Cloud services. Audio recordings are stored in Firebase Cloud Storage with access restricted to your account. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest.

5. Data Retention

  • Audio recordings are retained as long as your account is active
  • You can delete individual recordings from your history
  • Deleting your account removes all associated data
  • Cached AI-generated content (word of the day) is retained for 24 hours

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of push notifications
  • Export your analysis history

To exercise these rights, contact us at coming soon.

7. Children's Privacy

Sound Sharp is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes through the app or via email.
