Sound Sharp Support Privacy Terms

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Sound Sharp collects the following information when you use our app:

  • Account information: email address and display name (and the name Apple shares if you sign in with Apple)
  • Profile photo, if you add one (from your camera or photo library)
  • Audio recordings you make for analysis
  • Transcripts, scores and feedback for your answers
  • Voice measurements from your recordings: how much your pitch moves and how your volume changes, relative to your own voice (not a voiceprint)
  • Your interview setups (field, role and interviewer style; up to 3), and a company name and job posting for each if you add them. Your setups, question lists, company names and job postings are kept on your phone; only the setup in use (field, role and interviewer style) is stored with your account, along with the one used for each AI conversation or mock interview. The company and job posting are sent to Anthropic with your answers and question requests (with your current question list when you ask for a new set). They are not stored on their own, but questions and feedback written for that job, which can name the company, are stored with your results.
  • Jobs you add for a mock interview (up to 3): the job posting you paste or share, or the link you give and the job description read from it; the role, company, level and main requirements found in it, with your changes; and the practice rounds built for it, with their questions and results. Jobs are stored with your account so you can continue on another phone. This is separate from your interview setups, which stay on your phone.
  • An interview you pick from your calendar: that one event's start time and date, the job link in it, and the job titles (as written in the invite, or as you type them) and company email domain (such as example.com) of each interviewer, whether invited or listed in the invite. The app reads other people's names from the invite (the other people invited, the organizer and the people its notes list) and never sends them to Sound Sharp or Anthropic or stores them with your account. Interviewers' names, from the invite or as you type them, are shown only on the phone where you picked the event, and are kept only on that phone and in its own backups (iCloud or Google), if backups are on. The invite's notes also stay on your phone: the app reads them there to find the job link, the interviewers' job titles, whether the meeting is by video, phone or in person, and topic words from a fixed list (such as "system design", "case study" or "panel"), and sends only those details, never the notes. The event's title is sent after the app replaces email addresses with their domain (such as example.com) and links with "[link]", removes phone numbers and meeting PINs, and replaces the names it knows: interviewers' names with "Interviewer 1", "Interviewer 2" and so on, your own with "You", and any other (such as a guest who declined) with "Guest". Each interviewer is sent as "Interviewer 1" and so on, and the names the app knows are removed from their job titles. A name the app can't match, such as a nickname or someone the invite doesn't list, can be sent as written unless you change it. The title, the meeting format and the topic words are used to find the round type and are not stored. You see exactly what will be sent, and can change or remove it, before anything leaves your phone. Your other events are read only on your phone, to show you the list, and are never sent or stored.
  • Questions you type are stored with your answers, like any question you practice, and with any mock interview that asks them.
  • Your interview date, if you add one or pick an interview from your calendar
  • The one skill you're working on and how it's going, worked out from your results
  • Conversation answers and the AI's replies
  • Usage data: practice sessions, streaks and your time zone
  • Device push notification tokens, if you allow notifications
  • Subscription status and App Store purchase history

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Analyze your speech and give coaching feedback
  • Measure your voice (pitch movement and loudness) on our own servers to give you voice feedback. No AI service or other company receives anything for this, and we never use it to identify you.
  • Suggest interview questions and vocabulary
  • Track your progress and streaks
  • Keep track of the skill you're working on and pace your prep for an interview date
  • Send practice reminders and weekly summaries (if enabled)
  • Manage your subscription
  • Read the job postings you add, and build practice rounds and questions for that job and level
  • Tailor a round to the interview you pick from your calendar, and set your interview date from it
  • Count, across all users, how many people go on to a job's next round, to improve the feature

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it to train AI models.

3. AI Services and Your Permission

Before anything is sent to an AI service, the app asks for your permission. You can withdraw it at any time in Profile → AI processing; nothing more is sent after that.

  • AssemblyAI receives your voice recordings and turns them into text, with filler words and timing. We delete each transcript from AssemblyAI as soon as the analysis is done.
  • Anthropic (Claude) receives your transcripts and the questions you answer (including ones you type), interview setup (including any company and job posting you add), the jobs you add (the job description, its role, company, level and main requirements, the job titles of the people you'll meet, and a short summary of the job with each answer in its rounds), the details of an interview you pick from your calendar (its title, cleaned as described in section 1, whether it is by video, phone or in person, its topic words, and the job title and company email domain of each interviewer), conversation answers and short notes from your earlier answers (the skill you're working on and where you started on it, the advice you were given last time on the same question, your measured pace and filler rate, and which STAR parts that answer had), and writes your feedback, questions, practice rounds and replies. It doesn't receive the invite's notes, the email addresses in it, the names the app knows (see section 1), or your other calendar events. A name the app can't match, such as a nickname or someone the invite doesn't list, can be sent as written unless you change it. The invite's title can mention the date and time. Anthropic deletes what it receives within 30 days, unless it must keep it longer to enforce its usage policy or by law, and doesn't use it to train its models.

4. Information About Other People

A job posting or a calendar invite can mention other people, such as a recruiter or your interviewers. From an invite, Sound Sharp keeps the interviewers' job titles and company email domain with the job. Their names are shown only on the phone where you picked the invite, so you can see who you're meeting there. They are kept only on that phone and in its own backups (iCloud or Google), if backups are on, and the app never sends the names it knows to Sound Sharp or Anthropic. A name the app can't match, such as a nickname or someone the invite doesn't list, can be sent as written unless you change it (see section 1). The invite's notes, where other people are often named, never leave your phone; your other phones show only their job titles. Only their job titles are used to choose the kind of questions (for example a recruiter screen or a panel with an engineering manager), never to describe the people themselves. We remove email addresses and phone numbers from job postings, and from the page details and shared text that come with them, before they are stored or sent to Anthropic. We never look anyone up on the web or in other services, never contact them, and never use their details for anything else. Their details, including the names on your phone, are deleted when you delete the job or your account. Please add only your own interviews.

5. Other Services We Use

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, data storage, server functions, and notifications on Android
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and notifications on iPhone
  • RevenueCat: subscription management (your account ID and App Store purchase history)
  • Resend: sending sign-in code emails (your email address)
  • Mock interview questions and the word of the day are read aloud by your phone's built-in text-to-speech. On iPhone this stays on the device; on Android your phone's speech engine may use an online voice, depending on its settings. Only that text is spoken.
  • Job sites: when you give a link, Sound Sharp's server opens that one page to read the job description. The site sees a request from our server, not from your phone or your account. We never sign in to a job site; if a page needs a sign-in, or the site doesn't allow apps to read it (such as LinkedIn, Indeed and Glassdoor), the app asks you to paste the text instead.
  • Your phone's calendar: read on your phone, only when you choose an interview from it and only with your permission. Sound Sharp never asks you to sign in to a calendar account, never changes your calendars and never reads them on its servers.
  • Sharing to Sound Sharp: when you share a link or text from another app, Sound Sharp receives only what you shared, and keeps it on your phone until you open the app.

6. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase. Audio recordings are stored in Firebase Cloud Storage. The app plays each one through an unlisted web address given only to your account; anyone given that address could play the recording. Your profile photo is stored at an unlisted web address so the app can show it; anyone given that address could open it. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

7. Data Retention

  • Your recordings, results and history are kept while your account is active
  • Deleting your account removes all of them, including your recordings, profile photo and sign-in records
  • Transcripts are deleted from AssemblyAI once each analysis is done
  • Voice measurements are kept with each result and deleted with your account
  • The word of the day is cached for 24 hours
  • Jobs you add, with their rounds and calendar interview details, are kept until you delete the job, replace it with a new one, or delete your account
  • Interviewers' names from a calendar invite are kept only on the phone where you picked the invite and in that phone's own backups (iCloud or Google), if backups are on. They are removed from that phone when you delete the job or your account
  • Deleting a job removes it, its job description and its rounds from your account and your phones. Answers you recorded in its rounds stay in your history, like other practice, until you delete your account
  • From a link we keep only the job description, never the whole web page
  • We keep a record of the free first rounds you used in the last 30 days (run ids and dates only, no job details), even after a job is deleted
  • Anthropic deletes what it receives within 30 days (see section 3)

8. Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete your account and all its data at any time in Profile → Delete Account
  • Withdraw permission for AI processing in Profile
  • Turn off notifications in Profile or in your device settings
  • Delete a job at any time in Mock interview
  • Turn off calendar access at any time in your phone's settings (on iPhone: Settings › Apps › Sound Sharp). Jobs you already added stay until you delete them
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or ask any question about it
