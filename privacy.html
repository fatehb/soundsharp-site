Sound Sharp Support Privacy Terms

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Sound Sharp collects the following information when you use our app:

  • Account information: email address and display name (and the name Apple shares if you sign in with Apple)
  • Profile photo, if you add one (from your camera or photo library)
  • Audio recordings you make for analysis
  • Transcripts, scores and feedback for your answers
  • Voice measurements from your recordings: how much your pitch moves and how your volume changes, relative to your own voice (not a voiceprint)
  • Your interview setups (field, role and interviewer style; up to 3), and a company name and job posting for each if you add them. Your setups, question lists, company names and job postings are kept on your phone; only the setup in use (field, role and interviewer style) is stored with your account, along with the one used for each AI conversation or mock interview. The company and job posting are sent to Anthropic with your answers and question requests (with your current question list when you ask for a new set). They are not stored on their own, but questions and feedback written for that job, which can name the company, are stored with your results.
  • Jobs you add for a mock interview (up to 3): the job posting you paste or share, or the link you give and the job description read from it; the role, company, level and main requirements found in it, with your changes; and the practice rounds built for it, with their questions and results. Jobs are stored with your account so you can continue on another phone. This is separate from your interview setups, which stay on your phone.
  • An interview you pick from your calendar: that one event's start time and date, the job link in it, and the job titles (as written in the invite, or as you type them) and company email domain (such as example.com) of each interviewer, whether invited or listed in the invite. The app reads other people's names from the invite (the other people invited, the organizer and the people its notes list) and never sends them to Sound Sharp or Anthropic or stores them with your account. Interviewers' names, from the invite or as you type them, are shown only on the phone where you picked the event, and are kept only on that phone and in its own backups (iCloud or Google), if backups are on. The invite's notes also stay on your phone: the app reads them there to find the job link, the interviewers' job titles, whether the meeting is by video, phone or in person, and topic words from a fixed list (such as "system design", "case study" or "panel"), and sends only those details, never the notes. The event's title is sent after the app replaces email addresses with their domain (such as example.com) and links with "[link]", removes phone numbers and meeting PINs, and replaces the names it knows: interviewers' names with "Interviewer 1", "Interviewer 2" and so on, your own with "You", and any other (such as a guest who declined) with "Guest". Each interviewer is sent as "Interviewer 1" and so on, and the names the app knows are removed from their job titles. A name the app can't match, such as a nickname or someone the invite doesn't list, can be sent as written unless you change it. The title, the meeting format and the topic words are used to find the round type and are not stored. You see exactly what will be sent, and can change or remove it, before anything leaves your phone. Your other events are read only on your phone, to show you the list, and are never sent or stored.
  • Your resume, if you add one (one per account): the text of a PDF or Word file you pick. Before anything leaves your phone, the app reads the text there and removes your name; email addresses; phone numbers; your street address and postcode (ZIP code), and the other contact lines at the top of your resume, such as your city; web links and handles (such as LinkedIn or a personal website); the names and contact details of other people it finds, such as your references; and personal details such as your date of birth or age, nationality or citizenship, visa status, marital or family status, a parent's name, religion, caste, gender, health or disability, and ID numbers. Only the text is read: pictures in the file, such as a photo, are never sent, and a photo's caption is removed with your personal details. You see exactly the text that will be kept, with each removal marked, and can put back some removed details, change the text or cancel. When you change or add text, the app removes these details from it again, except a detail you put back. A detail the app doesn't recognize, such as a nickname or a name or number written in an unusual way, is kept as written unless you change it. When you confirm, the text is sent to Sound Sharp, which checks that it's a resume (if it isn't, nothing is kept) and removes email addresses and phone numbers the app missed, and web addresses that start with http or www or point to a known profile site, such as LinkedIn or GitHub. Your resume is then stored with your account, with the date you added it, whether it came from a PDF or a Word file, and whether only its first part was kept. From your work history our server also stores whether it shows a year or more between roles (or since your last one), and the fields of your roles, such as technology or healthcare (no dates, job titles or employers). That is used only to decide whether one round includes a neutral question about your path to this role, never for your fit summary, and is deleted with your resume. The file itself and its name are never sent. If you paste or share a resume where a job posting goes (Mock interview → Practice for a job, or an interview setup's job posting in Practice → Job Interview), the app recognizes it before anything is saved or sent and asks you first; it never uses that text as your resume, which you add as a file. If your phone doesn't recognize it as one, it's treated as a job posting. From Practice for a job it's sent to be checked (our server removes email addresses and phone numbers before the check) and isn't kept if our server finds it's a resume; if not, it's read as a job posting and kept only if you save that job. In an interview setup it's kept on your phone and sent with your answers and question requests, like any job posting you add there.
  • A fit summary for each job, once you've added a resume: for each of the job's main requirements, whether your resume shows it, partly shows it or doesn't show it, with a short reason (a requirement a resume can't show, such as the right to work, is marked as not assessed); an overall level worked out from those by a fixed rule shown in the app; up to 3 things to highlight in your answers; and one thing to prepare for each requirement your resume doesn't fully show. It's stored with the job, with the date it was made.
  • Questions you type are stored with your answers, like any question you practice, and with any mock interview that asks them.
  • Your interview date, if you add one or pick an interview from your calendar
  • Your goal, if you set one: what you're preparing for, the role if you add it, how many days a week you want to practise, and your interview date
  • The one skill you're working on and how it's going, worked out from your results
  • Conversation answers and the AI's replies
  • Live conversations: your voice while you talk with the AI, each of your answers saved as a recording of your voice, the AI's questions as text, how long the call lasted and how it ended, and how many live conversations you used today and this month
  • Usage data: practice sessions, streaks and your time zone
  • Device push notification tokens, if you allow notifications
  • Subscription status and App Store purchase history

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Analyze your speech and give coaching feedback
  • Measure your voice (pitch movement and loudness) on our own servers to give you voice feedback. No AI service or other company receives anything for this, and we never use it to identify you.
  • Suggest interview questions and vocabulary
  • Track your progress and streaks
  • Keep track of the skill you're working on and pace your prep for an interview date
  • Send practice reminders and weekly summaries (if enabled)
  • Manage your subscription
  • Read the job postings you add, and build practice rounds and questions for that job and level
  • Tailor a round to the interview you pick from your calendar, and set your interview date from it
  • Tailor your job rounds' questions to the experience your resume shows, and build each job's fit summary. The fit summary only compares your resume with the job's main requirements as listed in the app. It isn't a prediction of what any employer will decide
  • Count, across all users, how many people go on to a job's next round, to improve the feature

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it to train AI models. Your age, gender, nationality, family status, religion, disability or health are not used for your questions or your fit summary: labelled personal details, pronouns, graduation years and ages are left out before your resume is sent to the AI, and the AI is told to ignore any others your resume mentions and never to count a gap between jobs or a change of career against your fit. We never share your resume or fit summaries with employers or recruiters, and never use them to rank you against other people.

3. AI Services and Your Permission

Before anything is sent to an AI service, the app asks for your permission. You can withdraw it at any time in Profile → AI processing; nothing more is sent from that phone after that.

  • AssemblyAI receives your voice recordings and turns them into text, with filler words and timing. We delete each transcript from AssemblyAI as soon as the analysis is done.
  • Anthropic (Claude) receives your transcripts and the questions you answer (including ones you type), interview setup (including any company and job posting you add), your goal (what you're preparing for, the role and the interview date, if you added them), the jobs you add (the job description, its role, company, level and main requirements, the job titles of the people you'll meet, and a short summary of the job with each answer in its rounds), your resume, if you add one (the text you confirmed, after your phone removed the details listed in section 1, and without some personal details, such as a date of birth or the years you studied, which are left out before it's sent), the details of an interview you pick from your calendar (its title, cleaned as described in section 1, whether it is by video, phone or in person, its topic words, and the job title and company email domain of each interviewer), conversation answers and short notes from your earlier answers (the skill you're working on and where you started on it, the advice you were given last time on the same question, your measured pace and filler rate, and which STAR parts that answer had), and writes your feedback, questions, practice rounds, fit summaries and replies. It doesn't receive the invite's notes, the email addresses in it, the names the app knows (see section 1), your other calendar events, your resume file, or the details your phone removed from your resume. A name the app can't match, such as a nickname or someone the invite doesn't list, can be sent as written unless you change it, and so can a detail in your resume the app doesn't recognize. The invite's title can mention the date and time. Anthropic deletes what it receives within 30 days, unless it must keep it longer to enforce its usage policy or by law, and doesn't use it to train its models.
  • Google (Gemini) receives your voice during a live conversation, so the AI can hear you and reply out loud, and what the conversation is about: its topic, the question or scenario it starts from, and your interview setup's field, role and interviewer style (never a company, a job posting or your resume). It sends back its voice and the text of what was said. The app asks before your first live conversation, also if you have already given the permission above. Google keeps live conversations for up to 55 days to check for misuse and does not use them to train its AI. Your answers are then transcribed and coached by AssemblyAI and Anthropic, as with everything else you record.

4. Information About Other People

A job posting or a calendar invite can mention other people, such as a recruiter or your interviewers. From an invite, Sound Sharp keeps the interviewers' job titles and company email domain with the job. Their names are shown only on the phone where you picked the invite, so you can see who you're meeting there. They are kept only on that phone and in its own backups (iCloud or Google), if backups are on, and the app never sends the names it knows to Sound Sharp or Anthropic. A name the app can't match, such as a nickname or someone the invite doesn't list, can be sent as written unless you change it (see section 1). The invite's notes, where other people are often named, never leave your phone; your other phones show only their job titles. Only their job titles are used to choose the kind of questions (for example a recruiter screen or a panel with an engineering manager), never to describe the people themselves. We remove email addresses and phone numbers from job postings, and from the page details and shared text that come with them, before they are stored or sent to Anthropic. We never look anyone up on the web or in other services, never contact them, and never use their details for anything else. Their details, including the names on your phone, are deleted when you delete the job or your account. A resume can name other people too, such as your references, a manager or a client. Your phone removes the names and contact details of the people it finds before your resume leaves it; a name it doesn't recognize is kept as written unless you change it, so please check the text before you confirm. Please add only your own interviews and your own resume.

5. Other Services We Use

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, data storage, server functions, and notifications on Android
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and notifications on iPhone
  • RevenueCat: subscription management (your account ID and App Store purchase history)
  • Resend: sending sign-in code emails (your email address)
  • Mock interview questions and the word of the day are read aloud by your phone's built-in text-to-speech. On iPhone this stays on the device; on Android your phone's speech engine may use an online voice, depending on its settings. Only that text is spoken.
  • Job sites: when you give a link, Sound Sharp's server opens that one page to read the job description. The site sees a request from our server, not from your phone or your account. We never sign in to a job site; if a page needs a sign-in, or the site doesn't allow apps to read it (such as LinkedIn, Indeed and Glassdoor), the app asks you to paste the text instead.
  • Your phone's calendar: read on your phone, only when you choose an interview from it and only with your permission. Sound Sharp never asks you to sign in to a calendar account, never changes your calendars and never reads them on its servers.
  • Sharing to Sound Sharp: when you share a link or text from another app, Sound Sharp receives only what you shared, and keeps it on your phone until you open the app.
  • Files you pick: when you add your resume, you choose one PDF or Word file with your phone's own file picker. The app can open only that file, reads it on your phone, never sends the file or its name, and deletes its own copy once it has read it.

6. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase. Audio recordings are stored in Firebase Cloud Storage. The app plays each one through an unlisted web address given only to your account; anyone given that address could play the recording. Your profile photo is stored at an unlisted web address so the app can show it; anyone given that address could open it. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

7. Data Retention

  • Your recordings, results and history are kept while your account is active
  • Deleting your account removes all of them, including your recordings, profile photo and sign-in records
  • Transcripts are deleted from AssemblyAI once each analysis is done
  • Voice measurements are kept with each result and deleted with your account
  • The word of the day is cached for 24 hours
  • Jobs you add, with their rounds and calendar interview details, are kept until you delete the job, replace it with a new one, or delete your account
  • Interviewers' names from a calendar invite are kept only on the phone where you picked the invite and in that phone's own backups (iCloud or Google), if backups are on. They are removed from that phone when you delete the job or your account
  • Deleting a job removes it, its job description and its rounds from your account and your phones. Answers you recorded in its rounds stay in your history, like other practice, until you delete your account
  • From a link we keep only the job description, never the whole web page
  • Your resume is kept with your account until you replace it, delete it or delete your account. Replacing or deleting it removes it, and every fit summary made from it, from your account and your phones
  • A job's fit summary is replaced when it's rebuilt after you replace your resume, and deleted with your resume, the job or your account. A copy of each fit summary, which can quote your resume, is kept on your phones so you can see it offline, and so in each phone's own backups (iCloud or Google), if backups are on; a phone drops its copy the next time it connects after the summary is deleted
  • Questions from rounds you've started, which can mention your experience, stay in your history with that practice until you delete your account, even after you delete your resume or the job. Rounds you haven't started are prepared again without your resume after you delete it
  • We keep a record of the free first rounds you used in the last 30 days (run ids and dates only, no job details), even after a job is deleted
  • Anthropic deletes what it receives within 30 days (see section 3)
  • Google keeps live conversations for up to 55 days to check for misuse (see section 3). Your live conversation answers and their report are kept with your results, like other conversations

8. Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete your account and all its data at any time in Profile → Delete Account
  • Withdraw permission for AI processing in Profile (this also withdraws the permission for live conversations)
  • Turn off notifications in Profile or in your device settings
  • Delete a job at any time in Mock interview
  • See or delete your resume at any time, and replace it when adding a resume is available, in Profile → Your resume
  • If you withdraw permission for AI processing on a phone, nothing more is sent from that phone, so your fit summaries and questions stop being updated from it. Your resume stays with your account until you delete it
  • Turn off calendar access at any time in your phone's settings (on iPhone: Settings › Apps › Sound Sharp). Jobs you already added stay until you delete them
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or ask any question about it
