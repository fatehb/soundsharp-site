Sound Sharp Support Privacy Terms

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Sound Sharp collects the following information when you use our app:

  • Account information: email address and display name (and the name Apple shares if you sign in with Apple)
  • Profile photo, if you add one (from your camera or photo library)
  • Audio recordings you make for analysis
  • Transcripts, scores and feedback for your answers
  • Voice measurements from your recordings: how much your pitch moves and how your volume changes, relative to your own voice (not a voiceprint)
  • Your interview setups (field, role and interviewer style; up to 3), and a company name and job posting for each if you add them. Your setups, question lists, company names and job postings are kept on your phone; only the setup in use (field, role and interviewer style) is stored with your account, along with the one used for each AI conversation or mock interview. The company and job posting are sent to Anthropic with your answers and question requests (with your current question list when you ask for a new set). They are not stored on their own, but questions and feedback written for that job, which can name the company, are stored with your results.
  • Questions you type are stored with your answers, like any question you practice, and with any mock interview that asks them.
  • Your interview date, if you add one
  • The one skill you're working on and how it's going, worked out from your results
  • Conversation answers and the AI's replies
  • Usage data: practice sessions, streaks and your time zone
  • Device push notification tokens, if you allow notifications
  • Subscription status and App Store purchase history

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Analyze your speech and give coaching feedback
  • Measure your voice (pitch movement and loudness) on our own servers to give you voice feedback. No AI service or other company receives anything for this, and we never use it to identify you.
  • Suggest interview questions and vocabulary
  • Track your progress and streaks
  • Keep track of the skill you're working on and pace your prep for an interview date
  • Send practice reminders and weekly summaries (if enabled)
  • Manage your subscription

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it to train AI models.

3. AI Services and Your Permission

Before anything is sent to an AI service, the app asks for your permission. You can withdraw it at any time in Profile → AI processing; nothing more is sent after that.

  • AssemblyAI receives your voice recordings and turns them into text, with filler words and timing. We delete each transcript from AssemblyAI as soon as the analysis is done.
  • Anthropic (Claude) receives your transcripts and the questions you answer (including ones you type), interview setup (including any company and job posting you add), conversation answers and short notes from your earlier answers (the skill you're working on and where you started on it, the advice you were given last time on the same question, your measured pace and filler rate, and which STAR parts that answer had), and writes your feedback, questions and replies.

4. Other Services We Use

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, data storage, server functions, and notifications on Android
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and notifications on iPhone
  • RevenueCat: subscription management (your account ID and App Store purchase history)
  • Resend: sending sign-in code emails (your email address)
  • Mock interview questions are read aloud by your phone's built-in text-to-speech. On iPhone this stays on the device; on Android your phone's speech engine may use an online voice, depending on its settings. Only the question text is spoken.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase. Audio recordings are stored in Firebase Cloud Storage. The app plays each one through an unlisted web address given only to your account; anyone given that address could play the recording. Your profile photo is stored at an unlisted web address so the app can show it; anyone given that address could open it. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

6. Data Retention

  • Your recordings, results and history are kept while your account is active
  • Deleting your account removes all of them, including your recordings, profile photo and sign-in records
  • Transcripts are deleted from AssemblyAI once each analysis is done
  • Voice measurements are kept with each result and deleted with your account
  • The word of the day is cached for 24 hours

7. Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete your account and all its data at any time in Profile → Delete Account
  • Withdraw permission for AI processing in Profile
  • Turn off notifications in Profile or in your device settings
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or ask any question about it

To do this, email fatehb91@gmail.com.

8. Age Requirement

Sound Sharp is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
