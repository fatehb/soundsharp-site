How do I cancel my Pro subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Sound Sharp and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

I paid for Pro but the app shows the free tier

Open the app, go to Profile → Subscription and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Sound Sharp account and the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, recordings and analysis history. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it in Settings as described above.

How Sound Sharp reads job links

When someone gives Sound Sharp a link to a job posting, Sound Sharp's server opens that one page, once, at their request, to read the job description. It never goes back to the page on its own, follows the page's other links or crawls the site.

  • Its requests identify themselves as SoundSharp/1.0 in the User-Agent, with a link to this section.
  • It follows robots.txt, checked before the page and again on each redirect, and remembered for up to a day. A site can refuse it with a User-agent: SoundSharp group (for example, followed by Disallow: /); without one, the rules for all robots (User-agent: *) apply. If a site has no robots.txt, or it can't be read, the page is read.
  • Some job-board systems publish their postings through a public jobs API. For a posting on one of them, it reads that posting from the API instead of the page, without a robots.txt check; if that doesn't work, it reads the page as above.
  • It never signs in and sends no cookies or anything from the person's phone. The site sees a request from Sound Sharp's server, not from their phone or their account.
  • It doesn't read LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter or Wellfound pages, even through a redirect. For those, the app asks the person to paste the job description instead.
  • Only the job description read from the page is kept with the person's account (with the link and the job details they confirm), with email addresses and phone numbers removed, and only once they save the job. The page itself is never kept.
